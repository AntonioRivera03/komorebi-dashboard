# Komorebi — capability discussion notes

Proposal: 4 September 2026
Exported: 2026-09-05

These are discussion preferences, not an implementation specification.

## Essential to me

- A calm Today view (Direction): A small, explainable selection of what matters now.
- Goals that reach the calendar (Direction): Connect a reason to an outcome, a milestone, and a practical next action.
- One place to put things (Direction): Capture a task, thought, link or question before deciding where it belongs.
- A calendar that understands tasks (Direction): See fixed events alongside flexible work, with realistic buffers.
- A complete learning session (Learning): Read or watch, recall without help, get feedback, then apply.
- A knowledge library with provenance (Learning): Keep concepts, sources, your explanations, questions and projects connected.
- An exam preparation studio (Learning): Map syllabus coverage, practise under constraints and repair repeated mistakes.
- Japanese you can use (Learning): Combine recall with listening, reading and small speaking or writing tasks.
- A return ticket to any topic (Learning): Leave a small restart note when curiosity moves elsewhere.
- Unexpected connections (Learning): Occasionally suggest a useful bridge between things you already know.
- Rooms, scenes and honest status (Home): Control lights and climate, choose a scene, and see what actually responded.
- Only the home information that matters (Home): Bring attention to changes that need action; keep routine readings quiet.
- A voice doorway (Home): Use a short spoken request when your hands are occupied.
- Less recurring household admin (Life admin): Bring shopping, chores, renewals and maintenance into a quiet shared rhythm.
- A briefing with an attention budget (Information): A short digest tied to your commitments and interests, with sources and freshness.
- An assistant that can show its work (Information): Ask questions across your own material and preview proposed changes.

## Interested

- Review that fits your life (Learning): Schedule retrieval over time and limit new material when reviews accumulate.
- A financial awareness shelf (Life admin): Keep bills, renewals and self-defined savings goals visible when useful.

## Still considering

- Plan for the day you actually have (Direction): Choose available time and energy; get a smaller, feasible set of options.
- Routines that connect home and life (Home): Tie a chosen activity to a prepared environment and the right information.
- Running goals with a real baseline (Wellbeing): Connect your 5K and 10K intentions to an appropriate plan and honest check-ins.
- Flexible routines and fallback actions (Wellbeing): Give a routine a cue, an ordinary version and a smaller version.
- A weekly review with perspective (Wellbeing): Combine completed work with what felt meaningful, difficult or worth changing.
- People and experiences stay visible (Life admin): Keep chosen reminders for relationships, recreation and adventures.
