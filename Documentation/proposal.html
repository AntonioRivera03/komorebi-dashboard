02 / Make it tangible

The same life.
A different kind of day.

A dashboard should adapt to available capacity. Try these scripted examples; they demonstrate the interaction, not an AI planner.

Interactive concept · sample day

The rest of the day does not need to be filled. You can choose rest, dismiss a suggestion, or decide for yourself.

A little momentum

Begin with what matters.

  • Choose one meaningful action 5 min
  • Japanese recall and listening 15 min
  • Leave room between activities 10 min

Why this appears: a short language session fits your available time. The remaining time stays open.

When life interrupts

Replan once, gently.

Keep fixed commitments. Offer a smaller session or a new slot for flexible work. Show what changes before applying it, and stop repeatedly nudging after you decline.

When an interest returns

Restore the context.

Open the source, your last explanation, an unresolved question and the next useful exercise. You should not need to reconstruct why the topic mattered.

03 / Explore, then choose

The capability garden.

24 possibilities across six areas. Each has an everyday example, dependencies, a tradeoff and a way to judge whether it helps.

Foundation = my suggested essentials to discuss. Enhancement = useful depth once basics work. Experiment = promising, with more uncertainty. These labels are recommendations, not a build schedule; complexity is relative, not a time estimate.

06 / Technology carries the coordination

Quietly useful.
Easy to understand.

The strongest automations remove repeated setup, preserve context, or bring an exception to your attention.

Concept simulator: selecting a recipe changes the explanation below. It does not execute an automation.

When · trigger

You start learning

A deliberate tap on “Start session.”

Only if · conditions

Your setup is ready

You are home, the scene is enabled and the session has a linked source.

Then · action

Prepare the space

Open the source, apply the desk scene and start your chosen timer.

What this needsSession records and compatible home controls. Focus mode on other devices requires a separate supported integration.

What happens if it cannot runOpen the study source anyway; mark unavailable devices and retain manual control. Stop restores the previous scene only if you have not changed it yourself.

Home Assistant documents trigger/condition/action automations and execution traces. These examples combine that model with proposed learning and planning capabilities. Automation model ↗ · Traces ↗

Suggest

Keep judgment visible.

Offer a new time, flag a conflict or draft a learning prompt. Show the reason and let you edit or decline.

Act within chosen rules

Make repeats effortless.

A previously enabled lighting routine can run automatically. Keep a readable history, manual override and pause control.

Confirm consequential changes

Preserve your agency.

Ask before sending messages, spending money, changing external commitments or controlling sensitive devices. These should never be implied by a casual chat.

A useful future idea: after noticing repeated manual setup, offer “Would you like to make this a routine?” Show a plain-language preview and try it in suggestion-only mode before enabling actions.

Trust is part of functionality

Know what is current.

Attention is a limited resource

Let the system stay quiet.

09 / Why these ideas

Research notes.

Research and official documentation consulted on 4 September 2026. Evidence informs the capabilities; interface choices and automation recipes are proposals.

Research review · Dunlosky et al. · 2013Improving Students’ Learning With Effective Learning Techniques ↗

Supports practice testing and distributed practice as broadly useful learning techniques. It does not validate this proposed app or make every quiz effective.

Experimental study · Karpicke & Blunt · 2011Retrieval practice produces more learning than elaborative studying with concept mapping ↗

Supports retrieval for meaningful learning in the studied tasks. Concept links can help navigation, but a graph alone is not evidence of learning.

Meta-analysis · author-listed copy · Gollwitzer & Sheeran · 2006Implementation Intentions and Goal Achievement ↗

Supports specifying when, where and how to act. Our cue-and-fallback interface is a design interpretation, not a tested intervention.

Meta-analysis · Harkin et al. · 2016Does monitoring goal progress promote goal attainment? ↗

Progress-monitoring interventions improved goal attainment on average. This does not imply that tracking every area of life is helpful.

Product documentation · Anki ManualDeck options · FSRS and desired retention ↗

Describes adaptive scheduling and the workload tradeoff of higher desired retention. Useful precedent; integrating it requires a separate feasibility decision.

Official learning resource · The Japan FoundationWhat is Irodori? ↗

Uses practical communication objectives with listening and authentic or similar-to-real-life materials. An optional curriculum reference, depending on your purpose.

Public health resource · NHS · Better HealthGet running with Couch to 5K ↗

Describes a beginner progression of nine weeks or longer. Its endpoint is 30 minutes of continuous running; distance depends on pace.

Official documentation · Home AssistantUnderstanding automations ↗

Documents the trigger, optional conditions and action model. Our cross-domain recipes are proposed combinations, not prebuilt integrations.

Official documentation · Home Assistant · Developer docsIntegration manifest · IoT class ↗

Distinguishes local and cloud communication, including polling and push. Local operation must be checked per integration.

Official documentation · Home AssistantTesting and troubleshooting automations ↗

Documents execution traces and testing tools, informing the proposed “why did this happen?” view.

Official documentation · Home AssistantSet up a fully local voice assistant ↗
